Alice uses Whisper base (multilingual) for local Spanish speech recognition.
Whisper is an ASR component; Gemma remains the conversational model.

Runtime: ggml-org/whisper.cpp v1.9.2, official Windows CPU x64 package.
https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/releases/tag/v1.9.2
License: whisper.cpp-MIT.txt (MIT).

Model: OpenAI Whisper base, converted to ggml by ggerganov/whisper.cpp.
https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp
https://github.com/openai/whisper
License: whisper-model-MIT.txt (MIT).

Portable binaries and model weights are stored outside this repository.
Installation and pinned SHA-256 checks: scripts/testing/install-whisper-local.ps1.
Alice pipes WAV from RAM into whisper-cli and reads text from stdout.
No transcript/output-format flags are enabled and no audio files are created.
